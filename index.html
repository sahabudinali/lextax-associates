OUR PRACTICE AREAS

Comprehensive Legal Services

Professional legal support across a wide range of practice areas for individuals and businesses.

01

Corporate and Commercial Law

Legal assistance for businesses, companies and commercial transactions, including business disputes, partnership disputes, MSME matters and corporate advisory.

02

Contracts, Agreements and Legal Documentation

Drafting, reviewing and negotiating contracts, commercial agreements, legal notices, affidavits, applications, RTI applications and written submissions.

03

Criminal Law and Litigation

Bail applications, FIR quashing, criminal trials, appeals, revisions and defence representation.

04

Property and Real Estate

Assistance with property registration, title verification, transactions, GLADA and RERA matters.

05

Employment and Labour Law

Legal support relating to employment, workplace policies and labour law compliance.

06

Civil Law and Litigation

Civil suits, recovery suits, injunctions, property disputes, contractual disputes and civil appeals.

07

Matrimonial, Family and Consumer Law

Divorce, mutual consent divorce, maintenance, domestic violence, child custody, marriage registration, consumer complaints, deficiency in services and compensation claims.

08

Banking, Finance and Debt Recovery

Loan recovery, NBFC disputes, SARFAESI matters, cheque bounce cases and debt recovery proceedings.

09

Taxation, Regulatory and Compliance

Income tax notices, ITR disputes, tax recovery, assessment proceedings, statutory compliance, regulatory matters and legal risk management.
